Craft & Heritage

Understanding Malegaon’s Textile and Loom Heritage

Malegaon Exploring Editorial · 5 min read

Malegaon is widely associated with its textile and powerloom industry. Learning about this working heritage can add depth to a visit, but it is important to remember that workshops are workplaces.

Learn before you visit

Read a little about the powerloom industry and its role in the local economy so you can ask informed, respectful questions.

Respect workplace boundaries

Do not enter workshops or industrial premises without permission. Machinery and production areas can be hazardous to visitors.

Ask thoughtful questions

If a business owner is willing to talk, ask about the craft, products, and how the industry has changed over time.

Buy directly when possible

When appropriate, consider purchasing from local sellers and ask about the product’s materials and care.

Before you go: local timings, access, transport, and conditions can change. Confirm details with current local sources and follow local guidance.
